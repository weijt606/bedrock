# Assets

`Coca-cola.png` is the hero: the product sitting on the strata beneath it. It
ships with its own dark studio ground, so the page masks the outer edges away
rather than cutting it out — the metaphor only works if the rock reads as
photographed rather than as a sticker.

`Logo.png` is the mark alone; the wordmark is set in Figtree 800.

The six SVGs are the legend, one per stratum, in the order the dig answers them:

    circle-dot            what you see       the product
    flask-2               ingredients        what's inside
    factory               manufacturing      how it's made
    leaf                  planet impact      the cost to Earth
    legal-hammer-symbol   legal & ethical    is it compliant?
    circle-quarters       the bottom line    the full truth

`camera`, `microphone-alt-1` and `magnifying-glass-backup` are the three ways in.
